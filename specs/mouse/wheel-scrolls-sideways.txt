With -S a sideways wheel tick scrolls the view four columns, no further
than brings the widest row on screen and its newline to the right edge.
The cursor keeps its place: off screen to either side it is hidden
until a motion brings it back into view.

-- size --
6x3
-- args --
-S
-- input --
abcdefghijkl
xy
-- keys --
Right Right
-- mouse --
wheel right
-- screen --
efghij

<stdin
-- cursor --
hidden
-- mouse --
wheel right 3
-- screen --
hijkl

<stdin
-- cursor --
hidden
-- mouse --
wheel left 4
-- screen --
abcdef
xy
