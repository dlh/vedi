Releasing the button after a drag, a double- or triple-click or a
Shift+click copies the selection, as the terminal would have had the
pager not taken the mouse, and the status line says so. A click
selects nothing and copies nothing: the clipboard keeps the last copy.
:copy_on_select no stops it for the rest of the run; y still copies.
