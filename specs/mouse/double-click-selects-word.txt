Two clicks on the same cell within 400ms select the word there: the
letters, digits and underscores around the clicked one, on its line.
On a cell that is not in a word the cursor moves and nothing is
selected. Two clicks further apart in time are two clicks.

-- size --
40x5
-- input --
foo bar_1 baz
qux
-- mouse --
dblclick 0 6
-- screen --
foo [bar_1] baz
qux


<stdin>  line 1/2  wrap           h help
