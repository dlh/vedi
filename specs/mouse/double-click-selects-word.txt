Two clicks on the same cell within 400ms select the word there: the
letters, digits and underscores around the clicked one, with the
punctuation that joins them to the next word, so a path or a link is
one word, less punctuation at its end; a click on the punctuation
selects the same. Brackets and quotes never join. On a cell that is
not in a word, or on punctuation with a word on only one side, the
cursor moves and nothing is selected. Two clicks further apart in time
are two clicks.
