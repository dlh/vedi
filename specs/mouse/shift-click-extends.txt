Shift with a click extends the selection to that cell, from the
cursor when nothing is selected, as Shift with an arrow key does. The
anchor stays for the next one, and a plain click clears it.

-- size --
40x5
-- input --
one
two
three
-- mouse --
click 0 1 Shift+click 2 2
-- screen --
o[ne ]
[two ]
[th]ree

<stdin>  line 3/3  wrap           h help

-- mouse --
Shift+click 1 1
-- screen --
o[ne ]
[t]wo
three
