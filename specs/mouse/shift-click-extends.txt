Shift with a click extends the selection through that cell when it
lies past the anchor, and to it when before, as Shift with an arrow key
does: the anchor is a place between chars, the cursor's when nothing is
selected. It stays for the next one, and a plain click clears it.

-- size --
40x5
-- input --
one
two
three
-- mouse --
click 0 1 Shift+click 2 2
-- screen --
o[ne ]
[two ]
[thr]ee
