With the numbers at the left a click puts the cursor on the char under
it, and a click on a number on its row's first char.

-- size --
40x4
-- config --
line_numbers yes
-- input --
one two
three four
-- mouse --
click 1 8
-- cursor --
1 8
-- keys --
Shift+Right
-- screen --
1 one two
2 three [f]our
  ~
<stdin>  line 2/2  wrap           h help
-- mouse --
click 0 0
-- cursor --
0 2
