A click past a row's end puts the cursor on the newline, where End
would; on a wrapped row it stays on that row's last rune. A click below
the last line lands on the last row.

-- size --
10x5
-- input --
abc
abcdefghijklm
-- mouse --
click 0 7
-- cursor --
0 3
-- mouse --
click 1 12
-- cursor --
1 9
-- mouse --
click 3 1
-- cursor --
2 1
