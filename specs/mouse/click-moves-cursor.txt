A click puts the cursor on that cell and clears the selection, like an
unshifted arrow key. A click on the status line does nothing, nor does
a drag that starts there.

-- size --
40x4
-- input --
one two
three four
-- keys --
Shift+Right
-- screen --
[o]ne two
three four

<stdin>  line 1/2  wrap           ? help
-- mouse --
click 1 6
-- screen --
one two
three four

<stdin>  line 2/2  wrap           ? help
-- cursor --
1 6
-- mouse --
click 3 2 drag 3 2 0 0
-- cursor --
1 6
