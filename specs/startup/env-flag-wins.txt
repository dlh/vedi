A flag on the command line wins over the same one in VEDI, and the
rest of VEDI still applies: --wrap undoes its -S, and its tab width
stays.

-- size --
26x5
-- env --
-S --tab-width 2
-- args --
--wrap
-- input --
a	bcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz0123
-- screen --
a bcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxy
z0123
