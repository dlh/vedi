-F measures tabs at the terminal's stops, every 8 cells, whatever
tab_width says: the printed text is laid out by the terminal, not by
vedi. Two lines that fit at tab_width 1 would print as four rows, so
the pager stays.

-- size --
10x3
-- args --
-F
-- config --
tab_width 1
-- input --
aaaa	bbbb
aaaa	bbbb
-- screen --
aaaa bbbb
aaaa bbbb
<stdin>  l
