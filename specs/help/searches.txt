/ and ? search the bindings as they search the text: the match is
highlighted and scrolled into view, n and N step through the matches,
and a search that finds nothing or wraps says so in the banner's
place. A search starts from the screen as scrolled. The search is
help's own: it starts with no pattern, and the text's pattern, its
direction and its highlight are back on return.
