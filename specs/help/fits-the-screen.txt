The bindings are cut at the status line when the screen is too short
for them all.

60x6

abc

h
-- screen --
↑, ↓, ←, →, Home, End, j, k  Move the cursor
⇟, Space, f, ⌃F, ⌃V          Down a page
⇞, b, ⌃B, ⌥V                 Up a page
d, ⌃D                        Down half a page
u, ⌃U                        Up half a page
help  any key returns
