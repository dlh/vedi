A paste at the / prompt goes in as one line, its newlines dropped; the
paste is searched for, and Enter keeps the match.

-- input --
foo bar
bar
-- keys --
/
-- paste --
bar

-- screen --
foo {{bar}}
{bar}



/bar                                 1/2
-- keys --
Enter
-- screen --
foo {{bar}}
{bar}



<stdin>  line 1/2  /bar  1/2  wrap
-- cursor --
0 4
