The right edge of the prompt row counts the matches as the line is
typed: the one landed on and how many there are, 0/0 with none. After
Enter the summary row names the pattern after the line, / or ? for the
direction n goes, and the count, until Esc clears the highlight. n and
N step the count; from a cursor moved off its match, the match landed
on is counted again.

{fo}o
