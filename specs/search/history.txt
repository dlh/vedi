At the prompt, Up recalls earlier queries, newest first, and Down goes
back toward what was being typed. Editing a recalled query leaves the
history alone; a query equal to the newest entry is not added again.

-- input --
foo
bar
-- keys --
/ "foo" Enter / "bar" Enter / Up
-- screen --

{bar}
