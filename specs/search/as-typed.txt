Each character typed at the / prompt searches for the line from where
the prompt opened: the first match from there is highlighted apart and
scrolled into view, Backspace widens from there and not from the last
match, and a line with no match shows the screen as it was. Esc puts
back the cursor, the selection, the view and the pattern from before
the prompt; Enter keeps what the last keystroke found. ? does the same
backward.
