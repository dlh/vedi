At a prompt, a key that came with text types the text, unless the key
edits the prompt: Option+x on macOS types ≈, and Option+b moves back a
word, as Alt+b does, where it would have typed ∫.

-- input --
foo bar
-- keys --
/ "foo bar" Alt+b:"∫"
-- screen --
{{foo bar}}
~
~
~
~
/foo bar                             1/1
-- cursor --
5 5
-- keys --
Alt+x:"≈"
-- screen --
foo bar
~
~
~
~
/foo ≈bar                            0/0
-- cursor --
5 6
