wrap_width N wraps rows at N cells when the screen is wider, and a bar
in the cell after them, on every text row, shows where. w still
toggles wrap and nowrap, and :wrap_width 0 wraps at the screen's edge
again; neither has the bar.

-- size --
40x5
-- config --
wrap_width 20
-- input --
the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog
-- screen --
the quick brown fox │
jumps over the lazy │
dog                 │
                    │
<stdin>  line 1/1  wrap           h help
-- keys --
w
-- screen --
the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy



<stdin>  line 1/1  nowrap         h help
-- keys --
w : "wrap_width 0" Enter
-- screen --
the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy
dog


<stdin>  line 1/1  wrap           h help
