With wrap_style word, w toggles between nowrap and wrapping at words. A
word moves down with the space after it: jumps would fit the first
row, its space would not.

-- size --
25x5
-- config --
wrap_style word
-- input --
the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog
-- keys --
w
-- screen --
the quick brown fox jumps



<stdin>  line 1/1  nowrap
-- keys --
w
-- screen --
the quick brown fox
jumps over the lazy dog


<stdin>  line 1/1  word
