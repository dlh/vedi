With edge_markers yes and wrap_width N, the \ marker takes the Nth
cell, not the screen's last, and text wraps a cell early for it. The
bar follows in the cell after.

-- size --
40x5
-- config --
edge_markers yes
wrap_width 20
-- input --
the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog
-- screen --
the quick brown fox[\]│
 jumps over the laz[\]│
y dog               │
                    │
<stdin>  line 1/1  wrap           h help
