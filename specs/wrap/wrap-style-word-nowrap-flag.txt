-S starts in nowrap mode whatever wrap_style is; with wrap_style word,
w then wraps at words.

-- size --
25x5
-- args --
-S
-- config --
wrap_style word
-- input --
the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog
-- screen --
the quick brown fox jumps



<stdin>  line 1/1  nowrap
-- keys --
w
-- screen --
the quick brown fox
jumps over the lazy dog


<stdin>  line 1/1  word
