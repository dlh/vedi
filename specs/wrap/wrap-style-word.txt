wrap_style word in the config makes wrap mode break rows at spaces: a word
that does not fit moves whole to the next row, and the status line
shows the mode as word. Down moves by those rows, and a click past a
row's end lands on its last cell, the space.

-- size --
24x5
-- config --
wrap_style word
-- input --
the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog
-- screen --
the quick brown fox
jumps over the lazy dog


<stdin>  line 1/1  word
-- keys --
Down
-- cursor --
1 0
-- mouse --
click 0 22
-- cursor --
0 19
