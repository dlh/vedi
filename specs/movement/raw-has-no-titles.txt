With view_style raw a sequence that sets the window title is text:
stdin keeps its name and ] does not stop at the line. The second line
here starts with a raw OSC 2 sequence, ESC ] 2 ; t BEL.

-- size --
40x4
-- config --
view_style raw
-- input --
intro
]2;ta2
-- keys --
]
-- screen --
intro
[^[]]2;t[^G]a2

<stdin>  line 1/2  wrap  raw      h help
-- cursor --
0 0
