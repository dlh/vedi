Ctrl+Right moves to the end of the current or next word, Ctrl+Left to
the start of the current or previous one; a word is letters, digits and
underscores. At a line's end or start they cross to the next or
previous line. With Shift, each direction extends the selection.
Option (Alt) on an arrow does the same as Ctrl, and Option+f and
Option+b are the emacs word motions, which Terminal.app sends for
Option+arrow; those two never extend.
