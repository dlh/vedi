Alt+Down moves to the blank line after the cursor's paragraph, a run of
lines with text, or from a blank line after the next one; Alt+Up to the
blank line before it, or the previous one's. A line of spaces is blank.
With no blank line left, they go to the ends of the text. With Shift,
each extends the selection.
