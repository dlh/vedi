The end row after a trailing newline is one of the rows the text
fills: with three lines and that row on a screen of five, M takes the
third line, not the second, and B the end row.

-- size --
40x6
-- input --
l1
l2
l3

-- keys --
M
-- screen --
l1
l2
l3

B
