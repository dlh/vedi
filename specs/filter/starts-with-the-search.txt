& starts with the search pattern while its highlight is on, the list
already showing its matches; Ctrl+u clears it for another query. Once
Esc has cleared the highlight the prompt starts empty, and a parked
list comes back with its own query whatever was searched since.

-- input --
foo one
bar
foo two
baz
foo three
-- keys --
/ "foo" Enter &
-- screen --
{{foo}}[ one]
{foo} two
{foo} three


&foo                                 3/5
-- cursor --
5 4
-- keys --
Ctrl+u "ba"
-- screen --
{{ba}}[r]
{ba}z



&ba                                  2/5
-- keys --
Esc Esc &
-- screen --
[foo one]
bar
foo two
baz
foo three
&                                    5/5
-- keys --
Esc & "ba" Enter Enter / "foo" Enter &
-- screen --
{{ba}}[r]
{ba}z



&ba                                  2/5
