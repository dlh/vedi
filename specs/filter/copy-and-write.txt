The list is text: Shift+Down extends a selection over listed lines, y
copies them, and :write saves them, so the lines that match come out
as one.

-- input --
err one
ok
err two
ok
-- keys --
& "err" Enter Shift+Down y
-- screen --
[err one ]
{{err}} two



copied 1 line                     h help
-- clipboard --
err one

-- keys --
Esc : "write out.txt" Enter
-- screen --
{err} one
{{err}} two



wrote 2 lines to out.txt          h help
-- written out.txt --
err one
err two
