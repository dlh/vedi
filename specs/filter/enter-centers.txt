Enter on a listed line puts it in the middle of the screen, so what
comes before and after it is in view; near the text's ends, as near
the middle as the first or the last screenful allows. & brings the
list back with its query, which Ctrl+u clears for the next. The
screen as it is when Enter is pressed counts, not as it was when the
list opened.
