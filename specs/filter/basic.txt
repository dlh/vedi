& opens a prompt, and as the query is typed the text rows list only
the lines that match, the query highlighted and the pointed line in
reverse video; the right of the prompt row counts the lines listed
of the text's. Ctrl+n and Ctrl+p move the pointer. Enter keeps the list and
gives it the keys, and the status line names the query, the pointed
line of those listed and the text's line it is. Enter there goes to
that line in the text, highlighted as a search would leave it, so n
goes on to the next match.

-- input --
foo one
bar
foo two
baz
foo three
-- keys --
& "foo"
-- screen --
{{foo}}[ one]
{foo} two
{foo} three


&foo                                 3/5
-- cursor --
5 4
-- keys --
Ctrl+n
-- screen --
{foo} one
{{foo}}[ two]
{foo} three


&foo                                 3/5
-- keys --
Enter
-- screen --
{foo} one
{{foo}} two
{foo} three


&foo  2/3  line 3/5  wrap         h help
-- cursor --
1 0
-- keys --
Enter
-- screen --
{foo} one
bar
{{foo}} two
baz
{foo} three
