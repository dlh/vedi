At the & prompt, Up recalls earlier queries, newest first, and the
list follows; Down goes back toward what was being typed. A query is
remembered when Enter keeps its list, not when Esc leaves it.

-- input --
foo
bar
-- keys --
& "foo" Enter Esc & "bar" Enter Esc & "baz" Esc & Up
-- screen --
{{bar}}

-- keys --

-- screen --
{{foo}}
