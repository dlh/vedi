set_mark starts a selection at the cursor that every motion then
extends, Shift or not, as v does in vim and Ctrl+Space in emacs.
set_mark again ends it, the cursor staying, and motions move alone.

-- config --
map v set_mark
-- input --
abc
def
ghi
-- keys --
Right v Down Right
-- screen --
a[bc ]
[de]f
ghi
