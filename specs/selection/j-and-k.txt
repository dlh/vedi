J and K extend the selection by a line, as Shift+Down and Shift+Up do: j and k move
by a line, and the capital is the shifted form a terminal can send.

-- size --
40x5
-- input --
line 1
line 2
line 3
-- keys --
J J y
-- screen --
[line 1 ]
[line 2 ]
line 3

<stdin>  line 3/3  wrap  [copied 2 lines]
-- cursor --
2 0
-- keys --
K y
-- screen --
[line 1 ]
line 2
line 3

<stdin>  line 2/3  wrap  [copied 1 line]
-- cursor --
1 0
