Ctrl+Shift+End extends the selection to the end of the text and
Ctrl+Shift+Home to its start, where Ctrl+End and Ctrl+Home move. As
Ctrl+End leaves the cursor at the end of the last line, Ctrl+Shift+Home
from there takes the whole line.
