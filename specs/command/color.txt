color at the : prompt sets an element's style for the rest of the run,
as in the file: the selection underlined is no longer in reverse video.
A bad element or color is an error. Tab completes the element, then
each word of the style.

line 1
line 2
-- keys --
Shift+Right
-- screen --
[l]ine 1
line 2
~
~
~
<stdin>  line 1/2  wrap           h help

: "color selection underline" Enter Shift+Right
