Tab completes the last word at the : prompt: the command, then yes or
no, char or word, and an action name after map's key. One candidate
completes the word and a space, so the next word types straight on;
several, what they share, listed after the prompt as far as the screen
goes, starting late enough to show the one filled in; none, nothing.

-- keys --
: "g" Tab "7"
