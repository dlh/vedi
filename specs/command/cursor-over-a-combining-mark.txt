The prompt's cursor moves by the glyph: Left and Right step over a
character and the marks on it together, and Backspace deletes them
together, so a mark never lands on a character typed before it.

-- size --

-- cursor --

-- keys --
"x"

café

2 5

-- cursor --
2 6
-- keys --
