cycle SETTING at the : prompt sets the setting to the value after its
current one, the first after the last: wrap_style char becomes word,
and word char again. A setting the status line does not show,
edge_markers or auto_reload, is reported there with its new value.
Anything but wrap, wrap_style, edge_markers, file_separators,
auto_reload, status_line or view_style is an error.

-- size --
60x5

the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog, then naps in the sun
-- screen --
the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog, then naps in th
e sun


<stdin>  line 1/1  wrap                               h help
-- keys --
