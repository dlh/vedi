The prompt has a cursor, the terminal's, on the prompt's row. Left,
Right, Home and End move it; Ctrl+Left, Ctrl+Right, Alt+Left, Alt+Right,
Alt+b and Alt+f move it by word, and Cmd+Left and Cmd+Right to the ends
of the line, as in the text. Typing, Backspace and a paste act at it; a
recalled entry, like a completion, puts it at the end. The search prompt
is edited the same way.
